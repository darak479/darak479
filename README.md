### 👋 About Me

I'm an Analytics Engineer with 5+ years building data platforms — currently at **JPMorgan Chase**, previously **Charles Schwab** and **S&P Global**. My work centers on turning slow, manual, legacy data processes into governed, automated, self-serve systems that business teams can trust and query themselves.

I hold an M.S. in Business Analytics (Data Science track) from UT Dallas' Naveen Jindal School of Management, and my sweet spot is the intersection of engineering rigor and business usability: modeling data in dbt so it's testable and documented, orchestrating it in Airflow so it's hands-off, and shipping it into BI tools people actually use.

**What I care about right now:**
- 🔁 **Automation over manual work** — if I do something twice, it becomes a pipeline
- ☁️ Migrating legacy, on-prem databases to cloud-native architectures (Snowflake, BigQuery, S3)
- 🤖 Using AI/LLM tooling to speed up pipeline development, documentation, and code review — not to replace engineering judgment, but to remove the boring parts of it
- 📊 Governed, self-serve BI so analysts stop waiting on engineers for every new question
